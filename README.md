**About This Project**
This project was completed for LIS 5643: Introduction to Data Analytics at the University of Oklahoma in Fall 2025. It explored whether health and lifestyle indicators could be used to classify survey respondents into three diabetes status categories: no diabetes, prediabetes, or diabetes.

Using the BRFSS 2015 Diabetes Health Indicators dataset, we examined patterns through visualizations and trained a decision tree in R. The analysis included data preparation and a comparison of training and testing accuracy.

A key limitation was the uneven distribution of the outcome categories. Although the model achieved approximately 85% accuracy, this was similar to predicting the most common category for every respondent. The project therefore illustrates both the analytical process and the importance of interpreting performance carefully.

The project was completed by Azra Holland and Emily Satterfield. I took primary responsibility for rewriting and completing the R code. The accompanying report explains the methods and conclusions.
